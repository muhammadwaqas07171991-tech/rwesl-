Academic Engagements

Events & Lab Activities

Key academic exchanges, bilateral international delegations, scientific workshops, and community engagements led by RWESL members.

International Collaboration • Bilateral Exchange

Expanding Korea–China Collaboration in Agricultural Engineering 🇰🇷🇨🇳

Prof. Sang Min Kim, President of the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers (KSAE) and Head of RWESL, led a high-level academic delegation to the Chinese Society of Agricultural Engineering (CSAE).

The bilateral meeting fostered joint research in smart agricultural water management, watershed sustainability, and climate-resilient rural infrastructure.

System Launch & Milestone

Deployment of K-Water Guard AI Autonomous Agent

Official rollout of the autonomous water quality pipeline developed by Mr. Sangbin Ha and Dr. Muhammad Waqas under the guidance of Prof. Sang Min Kim. The agent provides real-time automated ingestion and spatial heatmaps across Korean watersheds.

Conferences & Academic Symposia

Annual KSAE Academic Conference Presentation

Presentation of recent breakthrough findings on deep learning potential evapotranspiration (PET) prediction in the Nakdong River basin and groundwater fluctuation modeling under water-curtain greenhouse agriculture.

Field Investigation & Survey

Namgang Dam & Nakdong River Watershed Hydrology Surveys

Comprehensive field water sampling and sensor calibration campaign conducted across major agricultural tributaries, feeding empirical validation datasets into our SWAT and HSPF hydrological models.
