Academic Engagements

Events & Laboratory Activities

Bilateral international delegations, international water forums, smart agriculture symposia, and academic outreach led by RWESL and the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers (KSAE).

International Bilateral Delegation • China & Korea

Expanding Korea–China Collaboration in Agricultural Engineering 🇰🇷🇨🇳

Prof. Sang Min Kim, President of the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers (KSAE) and Head of RWESL, visited the Chinese Society of Agricultural Engineering (CSAE) and China Agricultural University. They participated in the CSAE–KSAE Joint Symposium under the theme “AI plus AE (Agricultural Engineering)”, establishing vital exchange programs for smart agriculture and climate adaptation.

#KSAE #CSAE #AIplusAE #SmartAgriculture
International Policy Forum • Vientiane, Lao PDR 🇱🇦

3rd Asia Parliamentarians’ Water Forum (APWF) in Vientiane

Prof. Sang Min Kim participated in the 3rd Asia Parliamentarians’ Water Forum in Lao PDR as President of KSAE, alongside Korean National Assembly leaders and Ministry representatives. The forum hosted a joint academic session connecting Lao PDR universities and Korean water research societies to tackle transboundary watershed challenges.

#APWF #WaterSecurity #TransboundaryWatersheds
Bilateral Academic Exchange • Yanbian University

Signing Ceremony of Academic & Research Cooperation

RWESL and KSAE formalized a bilateral research cooperation agreement with Yanbian University Agricultural College. The collaboration focuses on cross-border agricultural water protection, joint telemetry data sharing, and researcher exchange programs.

#MOU #YanbianUniversity #AcademicExchange
Fieldwork & Research Seminar • Regional Watersheds

RWESL Summer Field Survey & Agricultural Water Quality Seminar

On the final day of August, the laboratory conducted intensive watershed inspections and field sampling across agricultural irrigation zones, deploying continuous telemetry loggers followed by a mid-year research progress seminar.

#Fieldwork #WaterSampling #Telemetry
Annual International Meeting • United States 🇺🇸

Participation in ASABE AIM Annual Meeting, USA

Lab Head Prof. Sang Min Kim participated in the ASABE Annual International Meeting, engaging in technical sessions organized by the Association of Korean Agricultural and Biological Faculty Engineers (AKABFE). He delivered remarks on future directions in agricultural water sustainability and attended student design competitions.

#ASABE #AKABFE #GlobalEngineering
National Water Policy • Osong, Republic of Korea 🇰🇷

2026 Rural Water Forum: 2nd Roundtable Meeting in Osong

Bringing together leading water scientists, agricultural engineers, and policymakers in Osong to analyze the water resources impact of the national Southwestern Semiconductor Cluster Development Plan and formulate strategies for securing clean rural agricultural water supplies.

#RuralWaterForum #Osong #WaterAllocation
National Agricultural Infrastructure Leadership

Meeting with President Kim In-joong of Korea Rural Community Corporation

Prof. Sang Min Kim held strategic talks with President Kim In-joong of the Korea Rural Community Corporation, establishing four joint initiatives: 70th Anniversary celebrations, Rural Development Act revisions, climate crisis water management solutions, and expanding the Rural Water Forum.

#RuralCommunityCorp #ClimateCrisis #PolicyCooperation
38th KSAE Presidency Inauguration • Year of Leap Forward

Official Inauguration of Prof. Sang Min Kim as President of KSAE

At the 2026 Regular General Meeting and New Year Ceremony, Professor Kim Sang-min was officially inaugurated as the 38th President of the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers. He declared 2026 as the “Year of Leap Forward,” launching the Future Vision Forum to link digital technology, smart agriculture, and regional resource management.

#PresidentialInauguration #KSAE70th #SmartAgriculture
