Flagship National Conference • Oct 28–30, 2026 • Lahan Hotel, Jeonju, Jeollabuk-do 🇰🇷
13 Special Sessions & Student Competitions

[2026 Korean Society of Agricultural Engineering Annual Conference Concludes Successfully]

Conference Theme: “Agricultural Engineering as a Convergence Platform: Data-Driven Decision-Making and Field-Oriented Implementation”

The ‘2026 Korean Society of Agricultural Engineering Annual Conference,’ held from October 28 to 30 at the Lahan Hotel in Jeonju, Jeollabuk-do, has concluded with great success.

The event was graced by numerous attendees from industry, academia, research institutions, and government. This year’s conference centered on the theme, “Agricultural Engineering as a Convergence Platform: Data-Driven Decision-Making and Field-Oriented Implementation,” fostering meaningful discussions.

Amidst complex challenges such as climate change and a shrinking rural population, the conference explored in-depth how cutting-edge technologies—including artificial intelligence (AI), big data, digital twins, satellites, and drones—can be applied to agricultural operations. The program featured 13 special sessions and a variety of student competitions. A highlight was the special lecture by Seong Je-hoon, President of the National Institute of Agricultural Sciences, titled “Can We Afford Not to Use AI?”, which was very well received.

Agricultural engineering must now evolve into a convergence platform that bridges knowledge and technology from diverse fields, facilitating rational decision-making based on accumulated data and ensuring effective implementation in the field.

We extend our deepest gratitude to Yang Oh-bong, President of Jeonbuk National University, who attended in person to share his valuable congratulations; to Han Jeoung-ae, Secretary-General of the Democratic Party of Korea (and Chair of the National Assembly Water Forum); and to Song Mi-ryung, Minister of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs, both of whom conveyed their congratulations via video and written messages. I would like to express my deepest gratitude to Kim In-joong, President of the Korea Rural Community Corporation (KRC), for serving as the moderator for the “KRC Policy Connection” (Gukjeong-ieum) forum; to Kim Woo-sang, Executive Director of the Infrastructure Division, for delivering congratulatory remarks during the special session on global cooperation; and to Choi Hyun-soo, Executive Director of the Water Resources Management Division, for moderating the special session on water and energy. I also extend my thanks to Lee Seung-han, Director-General of the Agriculture Policy Bureau of South Chungcheong Province; Lee Jae-cheon, Director of the Agricultural Infrastructure Division at the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs; and Official Kim Seong-ryul for their continued interest in and participation in our society. Finally, I would like to thank the leadership of the society and the professors from Jeonbuk National University for their dedicated efforts in preparing for this event.

Moving forward, the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers will continue to strengthen the collaborative network among industry, academia, research institutions, and the government—bridging the gap between cutting-edge technology and on-site needs—to contribute to the sustainable development of agriculture and rural areas. My sincere thanks go to all our members and everyone involved for being a part of this!

International Bilateral Delegation • China & Korea

Expanding Korea–China Collaboration in Agricultural Engineering 🇰🇷🇨🇳

Prof. Sang Min Kim, President of the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers (KSAE) and Head of RWESL, visited the Chinese Society of Agricultural Engineering (CSAE) and China Agricultural University. They participated in the CSAE–KSAE Joint Symposium under the theme “AI plus AE (Agricultural Engineering)”, establishing vital exchange programs for smart agriculture and climate adaptation.

International Policy Forum • Vientiane, Lao PDR 🇱🇦

3rd Asia Parliamentarians’ Water Forum (APWF) in Vientiane

Prof. Sang Min Kim participated in the 3rd Asia Parliamentarians’ Water Forum in Lao PDR as President of KSAE, alongside Korean National Assembly leaders and Ministry representatives. The forum hosted a joint academic session connecting Lao PDR universities and Korean water research societies to tackle transboundary watershed challenges.

Bilateral Academic Exchange • Yanbian University

Signing Ceremony of Academic & Research Cooperation

RWESL and KSAE formalized a bilateral research cooperation agreement with Yanbian University Agricultural College. The collaboration focuses on cross-border agricultural water protection, joint telemetry data sharing, and researcher exchange programs.

38th KSAE Presidency Inauguration • Year of Leap Forward

Official Inauguration of Prof. Sang Min Kim as President of KSAE

At the 2026 Regular General Meeting and New Year Ceremony, Professor Kim Sang-min was officially inaugurated as the 38th President of the Korean Society of Agricultural Engineers.
