## 🚀 How to Host Free on GitHub Pages (Recommended)

1. On GitHub (`github.com/muhammadwaqas07171991-tech`), create a new public repository:
   - Name: **`rwesl`**
2. Upload the files inside `rwesl_website/` to this new repository:
   - `index.html`, `k-waterguard-ai.html`, `publications.html`, `members.html`, `ongoing-research.html`, `events.html`, `gallery.html`
   - `assets/` folder (with `css/`, `js/`, and `images/`)
3. In repository **Settings** > **Pages**:
   - Set **Branch**: `main`, **Folder**: `/ (root)`
   - Click **Save**.
4. In ~1 minute, your website will be live at:
   - **`https://muhammadwaqas07171991-tech.github.io/rwesl/`**

## 🔗 How to Connect With Your Google Site

Once your GitHub Pages link is live, you can connect it to your Google Site in two ways:

### Method A: Full-Page Seamless Embed (Cleanest Look)
1. In your Google Site editor, open the page you want to update.
2. Click **Insert** > **Embed** > select **By URL**.
3. Paste: `https://muhammadwaqas07171991-tech.github.io/rwesl/`
4. Choose **Whole page**, and click **Insert**.
5. Drag the frame to fill 100% of the width and height.

### Method B: Header Portal Link / Banner Button
On your Google Site top banner or navigation, add a prominent button:
- **"Visit New RWESL Web Portal ↗"** pointing directly to your new hosted site.
