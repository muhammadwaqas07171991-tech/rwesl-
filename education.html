Lecture 01 Precipitation & Storms Active Module

🎬 Visualizing Depth–Area–Duration (DAD) Analysis in Hydrology 🌧️💧

Understanding how rainfall depth varies dynamically with storm duration (2h, 4h, 6h) and expanding spatial catchment area (1 to 20,000 km²). This 50-second scientific animation transforms mathematical storm physics into an intuitive learning breakdown with 7 analytical stages.

💡 Lecture 01 Key Takeaway:

Because storm precipitation attenuates with distance from the convective core, rainfall depth decreases as the catchment area increases. DAD curves quantify this decay curve to calculate Probable Maximum Precipitation (PMP), design flood hydrographs, and dam spillway dimensions.
