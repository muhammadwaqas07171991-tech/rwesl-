4 Modules (Lectures 01–04)

Basic / Elementary Hydrology

기초 수문학 (학부생 전공 기초 과정)

Foundational hydrologic curriculum for undergraduate engineering students. Topics include global catchment water balance, rain gauge measurement principles, spatial area-weighted precipitation estimation, Thiessen polygon geometric weighting, and storm Depth–Area–Duration (DAD) analysis. Includes scientific video lectures, slide decks (PPTX), and applied calculation workbooks (XLSX).
