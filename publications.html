Deep learning-based direct and indirect potential evapotranspiration prediction: A case of the Nakdong River basin, South Korea

Waqas M, Kim SM.

Investigates deep neural architectures for accurate evapotranspiration prediction across the agricultural sub-basins of the Nakdong River, South Korea. Demonstrates superior accuracy over classical empirical equations.

Waqas M, Kim SM.

Analyzes extensive seasonal groundwater fluctuations across high-intensity water-curtain greenhouse agriculture zones in Gyeongsangnam-Do, coupling statistical time-series decomposition with deep learning models.

Waqas M, Kim SM.
